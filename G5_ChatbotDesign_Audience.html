PART 2 · THINK LIKE A CODER 🔵

Pattern Recognition

A pattern is something that repeats in a way you can predict. Programmers spot patterns to write shorter code! 🧠

PLAY THE GAME 🎮

What comes next?

Shapes, then numbers, then both together!

IN PAIRS · 40 MIN ✏️

Make patterns for your partner

Shapes: draw a sequence (triangle, circle, star, triangle, circle, star…), then swap papers and describe your partner's pattern.
Numbers: write a sequence (2, 4, 6, 8… adding 2), swap again and find the rule.
Combined: mix them (triangle-1, circle-2, triangle-3, circle-4…), swap with a different partner and extend it.
🧠 Try a doubling pattern too: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16… What is the rule? (×2 each time!)

STEP 1 / 3

Find your buttons 🔎

Buttons are the parts the user clicks. First we need button pictures (called sprites).

Open Google and search for button sprite png.
Pick buttons that match your design (same style & colours).
Download the ones you chose to your computer.
💡 Choose PNG images — they have a see-through background, so they look neat on your chatbot screen.
STEP 2 / 3

Add the sprites to your project 📥

Open your chatbot project in mBlock.
At the bottom-right, find the Sprite panel and hover the round + button.
Choose Upload Sprite (the arrow-up icon), then pick the button image you downloaded.
Drag each button to a good place on the stage.
🖱️ Do this once for every button you need: Schedule, To-Do, the weekdays and the subjects.
STEP 3 / 3

Make them yours 🎨

Click a button sprite to select it.
Go to the Costumes tab (top-left, next to “Blocks”).
Use the Text tool (T) to write the button's name — e.g. “Sunday” or “Maths”.
Change the Fill colour, the size, and the design until you love it.
✅ Keep all buttons the same size and style — that is good design and good UX!
