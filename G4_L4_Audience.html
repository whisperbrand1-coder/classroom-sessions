PART 1 · HOW IT LOOKS 🖥️

User Interface (UI)

Everything you see and touch: the screen, the buttons you click, even voice commands.

UI is a bridge 🌉

⚠️ The most important rule: if the UI is not easy and simple, people will simply not use your app at all. Simple designs are always better.
SPOT THE DIFFERENCE 👀

Good UI vs Bad UI

Same app — two designs. Which one do you like? Tap the buttons to compare!

PART 2 · HOW IT FEELS 😊

User Experience (UX)

How users feel and how well they can actually use your app — making it easy, accessible and enjoyable.

So what is the difference? 🤔

UI

How it LOOKS

“How would the product look to the user?”

🎨 Colours · buttons · fonts · pictures
UX

How it FEELS

“How would the user feel while using the product?”

😊 Easy · fast · clear · enjoyable
😬 Careful! An app can have a beautiful design but still be impossible to use. Bad UX can stop people using your app completely — or even harm them. You need both.

REAL DESIGNER RULES 📐

Three tips for great UI & UX

✏️

Tip 1 · Sketch first

Draw your ideas on paper first — it is quick and easy to change.

🎨

Tip 2 · 60-30-10

60% main colour · 30% second colour · 10% everything else.

🔤

Tip 3 · Less is more

Never use more than two fonts in one design. Two is plenty!
