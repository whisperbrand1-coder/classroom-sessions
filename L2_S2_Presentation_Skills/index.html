THE WORD 🎣

So what is a hook?

A hook is the opening that makes your audience want to keep listening.

HOOKfirst 5–10 sec
PROBLEM SETUPthe rest of the opening
OPENING · about 30 seconds
⚠️ The hook is not the whole opening. It is the first few seconds. Then you set up the problem — and together those make your 30-second Opening.
THREE WAYS IN 🚪

Only three types. That's it.

❓

Question

Makes your brain search for an answer.

“What would you do with 15 extra minutes of your break?”

😲

Fact / Number

Surprises you with something unexpected.

“Students can spend most of their break in one queue.”

📖

Micro-story

Puts you inside a situation — you want to know what happens.

“Ahmed reached the front of the queue just as the bell rang.”

🚨 Never invent a number. If you cannot check it, use what you have actually seen instead. A made-up statistic dies on the first question.

But what happens after the hook?

Hook + problem setup

Problem + solution + benefits

30 sec③ CLOSING

Summary + call to action

“Test it for one week.”

CTA = Call To Action

It tells the audience what you want them to DO next.

❌

Weak

“That's our idea. Thank you.”

✅

Strong

“We want the school to test our pre-order system for one week.”

THREE QUICK THINGS ⚡

Audience · Delivery · Team flow

👥 Same idea, different words

🎒

Students care about

Time · convenience · experience
“Get your whole break back.”

🧑‍🏫

The principal may care about

Impact · feasibility · resources · school improvement
“No extra staff time needed.”

🎯 Ask your team right now: would your final hook work for BOTH audiences? If not — which one are you choosing?
