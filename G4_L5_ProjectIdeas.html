16 Presentation Ideas
GRADE 4 · SESSION 3 · LESSON 5 💡

Pick an app. Build the slides.

Sixteen ready-made plans for your final presentation. Every one is about an app you already use, and every one tells you exactly what goes on all six slides.

📋 How to use this page

  1. Choose one app with your group. Tap it to open the plan.
  2. Open PowerPoint → New → blank presentation.
  3. Make 6 slides. Follow the plan one slide at a time — do not skip ahead.
  4. 🔍 blue box = a picture to search for. ⌨️ green box = words to type.
  5. Where you see ____ , that is your own idea. Fill it in yourself.
  6. You have 5 minutes to present. Six slides means about 50 seconds each — enough time to actually explain, not just read.
