The Smart Device Command Center

You are embedded systems engineers. Your manager left the cards scrambled. Sort every one into its correct zone before the product launch.

📋 How to play

  1. Tap a card to pick it up, then tap the box where you think it belongs.
  2. Changed your mind? Tap the red ✕ on a placed card to take it back out — or tap the card and then tap a different box to move it straight there.
  3. Place all 24 cards across the five zones.
  4. Then scroll to the bottom and ask your facilitator to unlock the answers.

💡 Two questions that solve most of this: for Zone 1 — does the outcome need BOTH conditions, just ONE, or the OPPOSITE? For Zones 4 and 5 — does this item sense, decide, or act?

🔒 Check your answers

Only your facilitator has the password. Place every card first — you will not be told what is right or wrong until it is unlocked.
